Strategic Framework

Demand Creation vs Demand Capture

Demand CREATION
Putting Brady Elizabeth into consideration before someone Googles "hotel Melbourne".
Tactics:
  • 📍 Founders' Club narrative (story + scarcity)
  • 📍 Micro-creator content network
  • 📍 YouTube TrueView + IG Reels
  • 📍 PR + earned editorial
  • 📍 Event content angles
Recommended split: ~55% of $100K
Demand CAPTURE
Converting people already searching for a Melbourne hotel into Brady Elizabeth bookings.
Tactics:
  • 📍 Brand search (defence)
  • 📍 Generic "hotel Melbourne" Search
  • 📍 PMAX (intent-led)
  • 📍 Metasearch (Google · Trivago · TripAdvisor)
  • 📍 Display + Social retargeting
Recommended split: ~45% of $100K
The trap: Most launch strategies are 90% capture · 10% creation → "Be visible everywhere, hope demand converts." That fails in Melbourne. Our shift: Front-load creation in months 1–3, then ramp capture as branded demand appears in search.
