What Works · What's Next

Creative Roadmap — May 2026 Onward

① Keep "Saver Rate" theme running across all 4 Hotel Sales: Apr CTR 2.60% (vs 1.19% legacy). Already replaced all 32 old "Book Direct & Save" / "Book Early" creatives — keep them. Refresh asset pool every 4–6 weeks to prevent fatigue.
② Scale UGC Video format aggressively: 3.36% blended CTR · works for Saver Rate, Brand, and Events angles. Brief 5–8 new UGC creators for May (vertical 9:16, 10–15s). Replace static creative across Brady Group Sales Daily campaign.
③ Pause all 16 Autumn Sale Static Story ads: 0.43% CTR average across 4 hotels — concept fatigue + static format underperforming. $552/month wasted. Reallocate to UGC Video scaling.
④ Test next: Saver Rate as UGC Video — combine the two winners. The "Saver Rate" message is converting; vertical UGC format is engaging. Expected uplift: 4%+ CTR on retarget audiences.
⑤ Establish creative refresh cadence: Every theme launches with a clear date suffix (e.g. "Theme | MMM 'YY") — keep the discipline. Makes before/after analyses repeatable and isolates creative impact from media-mix changes.
